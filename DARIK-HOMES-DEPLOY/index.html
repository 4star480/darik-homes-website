  1. 01 · The plot

    Kaura District, Abuja

    Plot 797, Cadastral Zone B11, after the second gate of Prince and Princess Estate. The building is set out on the ground before anything is dug.

  2. 02 · Foundation

    Structure comes first

    Footings go in under every column, then the ground slab. Our mission is buildings with adequate structural stability and great economic satisfaction.

  3. 03 · Frame

    Reinforced concrete, floor by floor

    Columns and slabs rise for three floors of flats, with timber scaffolding around the frame, the way it is done on site.

  4. 04 · Blockwork

    Walls closed in

    Blockwork fills the frame and leaves the openings for windows, doors and balconies.

  5. 05 · Finishes

    White render, charcoal accents

    Balcony frames, glazing and the two-tone facade you can see on site today. The scaffolding comes down last.

  6. 06 · Gated

    Walled and gated

    A perimeter wall with charcoal pillars and slatted infill, a gate and a gatehouse. The design paves the courtyard.
