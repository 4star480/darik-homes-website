Darik Homes Limited is a real estate development and construction management company committed to structural stability and economic satisfaction in high quality buildings and civil engineering structures.

Who we are

Developers who build.

As a real estate development and general construction company, we focus on bringing modern technology into residential and commercial buildings, using sustainable methods of construction.

Led by a team of professionals in the built environment, we develop and sell properties in strategic places within Nigeria, and we take on general construction and renovation work for clients.
